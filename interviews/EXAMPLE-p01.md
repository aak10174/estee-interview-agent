# Interview P01 — 22, Shanghai, university student
(Example file so you can test the pipeline before real interviews arrive.
Delete it once you have real transcripts.)

**Interviewer:** Where do you usually hear about a new skincare or makeup product?

**P01:** Honestly Xiaohongshu. I don't watch ads. If three different people I follow
post about the same serum in one week I'll look it up. My mum buys from the counter
at the department store but I've never bought anything that way.

**Interviewer:** What do you think of Estée Lauder?

**P01:** It's my mum's brand. The brown bottle, everyone knows it. It's good quality
I think, but it feels like something you get given, not something you choose. When I
see the counter it looks like a bank.

**Interviewer:** What do you buy instead?

**P01:** Mostly Chinese brands now — Florasis for makeup, Proya for skincare. Proya
is like a third of the price and the ingredient list is right on the front. And they
actually talk to you like a person online.

**Interviewer:** Is price the main thing?

**P01:** Not exactly. I spent 600 yuan on a Lancôme foundation last year because a
friend let me try hers and it matched. I'll pay if I'm sure. What I won't do is pay
600 for something I've only seen in a photo. The risk is the problem, not the price.

**Interviewer:** Would anything make you consider Estée Lauder?

**P01:** If they made something that was obviously for me and not for my mum. And
samples. If I could try it without committing, honestly I'd probably buy it.
